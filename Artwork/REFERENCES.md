# Wallpaper artwork

All six wallpapers were generated individually with the built-in ImageGen tool for Driftwood. The online references informed the lighting and seasonal atmosphere. The app bundles the new illustrations.

- [KoiArt’s desktop weather demos](https://koiart.app/#effects)
- [Thomas Kinkade Studios’ winter art gallery](https://www.thomaskinkade.com/pages/browse-winter-art)
- [The Fitzwilliam Museum’s Snow Country exhibition](https://snowcountry.fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/explore/journeys-through-snow%E2%80%93mountain-and-river)

## Generation prompt

Create one full-bleed landscape desktop wallpaper, wide 16:9 composition, an original wholesome hand-painted storybook environment. [Scene below.] Rich gouache texture, luminous color contrast, painterly brushwork, layered atmospheric perspective and thoughtful lived-in details. Keep center and top clear enough for desktop UI and avoid extremely busy texture. No words, borders, interface, logos, collages, frames or snowmen. Let the app overlay supply active weather. Original art inspired by the broad visual tradition of illuminated cottages and quiet seasonal woodcut landscapes. Output one landscape image.

- **Snow Lantern Inn**. A welcoming timber mountain inn with warm golden windows on the right third, indigo snowy pines, a curved lantern path with footprints through violet-blue snow, two tiny distant travelers, low rocky mountains under a deep cobalt winter sky. Cabin with green shutters, distinctly original composition.
- **The Rainy Bookshop**. A corner secondhand bookshop and its amber windows in a tiny hillside town at rainy blue hour, dark teal slate roofs, wet cobblestones with coral and amber reflections, a small figure with yellow umbrella on the right, flowerpots and a sleeping cat under awning.
- **Blossom Creek**. A peach plaster cottage and a tiny stone bridge over a winding turquoise creek at spring sunrise, blooming pale pink orchard, flower meadow, ducks on water, warm yellow light through windows, hazy green hills.
- **Cider Mill**. A cozy cider mill with waterwheel next to a stream, weathered timber and glowing windows, copper and crimson maple groves, little footbridge and pumpkins in foreground, honey sunset after a crisp autumn day.
- **Lantern Marsh**. A small round-roofed lantern keeper cottage on the edge of a teal marsh, curved wooden boardwalk, lilypads, reeds, tiny floating golden lights, green blue twilight and luminous reflections.
- **Campfire Lake**. Two warm tents beside a violet alpine lake, little campfire, a person stargazing, silhouettes of evergreen trees, an expansive indigo Milky Way and luminous peaks reflected in still water.
